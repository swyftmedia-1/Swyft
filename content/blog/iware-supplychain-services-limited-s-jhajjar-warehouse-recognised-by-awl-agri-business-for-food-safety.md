iWare Supplychain Services Limited has received recognition from AWL Agri Business for its Jhajjar warehouse in Haryana, marking an important milestone in the company’s commitment to food safety, warehouse management and operational excellence.

The recognition highlights the importance of maintaining consistent operational standards across food-related supply chains, where safe handling, process discipline and efficient warehouse operations play a crucial role.

The Jhajjar facility, operated by iWare Supplychain Services Limited, supports warehousing requirements associated with AWL Agri Business. This recognition underscores the value of disciplined daily operations and meeting customer expectations in a sector where food safety remains a priority.
Focus on Food Safety and Operational Excellence
Food safety is an essential component of modern warehousing and supply chain management. Facilities handling food products must maintain appropriate hygiene practices, follow established procedures and ensure that operational processes support the safe storage and movement of goods.

Recognition from AWL Agri Business reinforces the importance of these practices and highlights the role of reliable warehousing operations in supporting the wider supply chain.

For logistics service providers, maintaining operational consistency requires effective coordination, attention to detail and the collective efforts of teams across all levels of warehouse operations.
Recognition for the Team Behind the Operations
The achievement also acknowledges the people responsible for the day-to-day functioning of the Jhajjar warehouse. From warehouse management to on-ground execution, teamwork and a shared commitment to operational standards are essential to delivering dependable services.

For iWare Supplychain Services Limited, the recognition serves as encouragement to continue strengthening operational practices and maintaining service standards for its customers.
iWare Supplychain Services Limited and India’s Warehousing Sector
Part of Inter India Group, iWare Supplychain Services Limited operates in the warehousing and supply chain services sector, supporting businesses with their logistics and storage requirements.

As Indian businesses place increasing importance on supply chain reliability, warehousing facilities that prioritise safety, operational discipline and service quality remain an important part of the logistics ecosystem.

The recognition of iWare’s Jhajjar warehouse by AWL Agri Business marks an important achievement for the company and its team, reinforcing the value of consistent execution and a continued focus on operational excellence.
