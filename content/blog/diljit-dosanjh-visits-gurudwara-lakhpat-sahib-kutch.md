Diljit Dosanjh Visits Historic Gurudwara Lakhpat Sahib in Kutch, Meets Devotees and Locals
Kutch, Gujarat: Punjabi singer and actor Diljit Dosanjh visited the historic Gurudwara Lakhpat Sahib in Kutch, Gujarat, where he paid obeisance and interacted with devotees and local residents.

His visit brought attention to this historic Sikh religious site, which holds immense spiritual and historical significance. Gurudwara Lakhpat Sahib is associated with the first Sikh Guru, Sri Guru Nanak Dev Ji, and remains an important destination in Sikh religious heritage.

During his visit, Diljit Dosanjh met devotees and local residents gathered at the Gurudwara, sharing moments of interaction at this historic spiritual destination.

Located in the border region of Kutch, Gurudwara Lakhpat Sahib is an important part of Gujarat's cultural and religious heritage, attracting devotees and visitors from different parts of the country.
